**Answer:** Jonah Bell.

**Key idea.** Facts 1 and 2 name the same two people. Fact 3 rules one of them out.

1. Fact 1: the person behind the Affair is the answer to Puzzle 96 (Teddy) or Puzzle 98 (Jonah).
2. Fact 2: the same person rowed to the Point, which again means Teddy (Puzzle 97) or Jonah (Puzzle 99). Both are still possible.
3. Fact 3 settles it. Teddy snapped the 9 p.m. photo on the green (Puzzle 100), so he could not have been in the lantern room at nine.
4. The only person who held the key, rowed to the Point, and could be at the lamp at 9 p.m. is Jonah Bell.

*What really happened:* Ada and her apprentice find Jonah at his ferry workshop with the lamp, its old reflector freshly re-silvered. The three blinks were his great-grandfather Silas Bell's "all's well" signal. The logbook went out to "S. Bell" on Silas's old library card, as a private joke. The flickers and the moved dinghy were Jonah's practice runs, the polished brass in November his first try at cleaning it, and his ferry, it turns out, is named the *Silas B.* That night the Keeper's Lamp gets its 150th-birthday lighting on time and shines brighter than anyone in Thimble Harbor can remember. Ada allows herself one exclamation mark: "Case closed, partner!"
