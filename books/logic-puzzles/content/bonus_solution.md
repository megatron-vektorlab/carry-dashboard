**Answer:** Jonah Bell.

**Key idea.** Each fact names two people. Find the one person who appears on both lists and is not ruled out.

1. Fact 1: the person behind the Affair is the answer to Puzzle 96 (Teddy) or Puzzle 98 (Jonah).
2. Fact 2: the same person rowed to the Point, which again means Teddy (Puzzle 97) or Jonah (Puzzle 99). Both are still possible.
3. Fact 3 settles it. Teddy snapped the 9 p.m. photo on the green (Puzzle 100), so he could not have been in the lantern room at nine.
4. The only person who held the key, rowed to the Point, and could be at the lamp at 9 p.m. is Jonah Bell.

*What really happened:* Ada and her apprentice find Jonah at his ferry workshop with the lamp, its old reflector freshly re-silvered. The three blinks were his great-grandfather Silas Bell's "all's well" signal. "S. Bell" on the library's logbook was Silas's old library card, borrowed as a private joke, and the flickers and the moved dinghy were Jonah's practice runs. That night the Keeper's Lamp is relit on time and shines brighter than anyone in Thimble Harbor can remember. Ada allows herself one exclamation mark: "Case closed, partner!"
