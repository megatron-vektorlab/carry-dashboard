## Rights & pricing

* **Territories: Individual territories — select all except the United Kingdom.**
  In the UK the King James text is under Crown copyright (letters patent,
  administered by Cambridge University Press). Their free permission covers only
  non-commercial use, so a commercial puzzle book needs written permission
  (permissions@cambridge.org). Add the UK later if they grant it, then set
  `"uk_permission": true` in `data/release.json` and rebuild (the copyright page
  then prints Cambridge's required acknowledgement).
* Royalty: 60 %.
* Suggested list price: **⟨PRICE_USD⟩** (EU ⟨PRICE_EUR⟩ ex VAT, CA ⟨PRICE_CAD⟩, AU ⟨PRICE_AUD⟩).
* Royalty estimate (US): ⟨PAGES⟩ pages → print cost $1.00 + $0.017 × ⟨PAGES⟩ = **⟨PRINT_COST⟩**;
  60 % × ⟨PRICE_USD⟩ − ⟨PRINT_COST⟩ ≈ **⟨ROYALTY⟩ per sale**. KDP's pricing calculator is authoritative.
* Expanded distribution: off (royalty would be near zero at this price).
